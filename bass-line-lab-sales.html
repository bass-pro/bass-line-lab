Write to me directly. I'll work through it with you.

The ideal is twice a week, 45 minutes each: watch the lesson, then practice it. If you have more time, use it. If you have less, you get lifetime access and go at your own pace.
What genre is this for?
All of them. The shapes work in rock, soul, gospel, jazz. Once you know them, you apply them anywhere.
I've been playing for years and I'm still stuck. Will this actually help?
That's exactly who this is for. Not beginners. Players who put in the time and still hit a ceiling. 16th notes are what's missing. Once you have them, the ceiling goes.
Am I at the right level for this?
This is for bass players who already know the basics. I included the Essential Rhythms Mini Course as a bonus, so if you're a beginner and enthusiastic, start there and work your way up.
